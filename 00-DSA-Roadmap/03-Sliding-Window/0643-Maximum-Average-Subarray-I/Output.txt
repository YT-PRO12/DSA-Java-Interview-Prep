========================================
          MAXIMUM AVERAGE SUBARRAY I
========================================

Problem:
Find the maximum average of any subarray of size K.

Input:
Array = [1, 12, -5, -6, 50, 3]
K = 4

Output:
12.75

Explanation:
The subarray [-6, 50, 3, 12] is not contiguous in this input.
The maximum-sum window is [12, -5, -6, 50].

Sum = 51
Average = 51 / 4 = 12.75
