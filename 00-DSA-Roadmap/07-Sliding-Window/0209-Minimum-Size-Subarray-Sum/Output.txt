========================================
          MINIMUM SIZE SUBARRAY SUM
========================================

Problem:
Find the minimum length of a contiguous subarray
whose sum is greater than or equal to the target.

Input:
Target = 7
Array = [2, 3, 1, 2, 4, 3]

Output:
2

Explanation:
The subarray [4, 3] has a sum of 7.

Length = 2
